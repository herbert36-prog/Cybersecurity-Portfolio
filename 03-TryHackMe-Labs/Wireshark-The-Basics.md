## Personal Reflection

I found this room useful for developing my understanding of network packet analysis, although I initially found the amount of information displayed in Wireshark overwhelming.

When examining packet captures, there was a lot of information being presented at once. To make this easier to understand, I used colour filters to distinguish different sections and types of traffic. This helped me organise the information visually and focus on the packets I wanted to investigate.

Another challenge was understanding hexadecimal values when examining packet contents. I found it difficult to interpret some of the information because I did not fully understand how hexadecimal related to binary.

To improve my understanding, I went back and revised binary number representation before continuing with the packet analysis exercises. This helped me understand how hexadecimal values represent binary data and why both formats are useful when examining packets.

This experience taught me the importance of understanding fundamental computing concepts when working with more advanced cybersecurity tools.

Overall, I found the room valuable because it strengthened my understanding of packet analysis, network protocols and the importance of filtering information during an investigation. It also helped me recognise that being able to interpret the information presented by a security tool is just as important as knowing how to operate it.
