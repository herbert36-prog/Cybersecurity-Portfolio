Having this practical experience helped me relate the concepts covered in the room to real networking scenarios. Rather than just learning definitions, I could connect the material to configurations and troubleshooting exercises I had already completed in Packet Tracer.

This room reinforced my interest in networking and helped strengthen my understanding of networking fundamentals. I particularly enjoy the practical side of networking, where I can build networks, configure devices and troubleshoot problems.
