# TryHackMe – SOC L1 Alert Triage

## Room Information

- **Platform:** TryHackMe
- **Room:** SOC L1 Alert Triage
- **Room link:** https://tryhackme.com/room/socl1alerttriage
- **Status:** Completed

## Overview

This room introduced me to the alert triage process within a Security Operations Centre (SOC).

The practical exercises helped me understand how security alerts are reviewed, investigated, classified and directed to the appropriate teams.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the purpose of security alert triage.
- Review and investigate security alerts.
- Understand alert classification.
- Recognise the importance of escalation.
- Understand the responsibilities of different SOC teams.

## Practical Experience

During this room, I worked through the alert triage process and developed an understanding of how alerts are handled within a SOC environment.

## Key Learning Points

### Alert Investigation

Understanding the importance of reviewing an alert and gathering relevant information before deciding what action should be taken.

### Alert Classification

Understanding that alerts need to be assessed and categorised based on the available evidence.

### Escalation and Team Responsibilities

Understanding that different security issues may need to be handled by different departments or specialist teams.

## Challenges Encountered

The main difficulty I experienced was understanding which department or team was responsible for handling particular alerts.

Although I found the general investigation process relatively straightforward, I needed to improve my understanding of how alerts should be directed to the appropriate team.

## Personal Reflection

I found this room useful because it introduced me to the responsibilities of a Level 1 SOC analyst.

It helped me understand that security analysts need more than technical investigation skills. They also need to understand escalation procedures, communicate their findings and know when an issue needs to be passed to another team.

The experience highlighted an area I would like to improve: understanding the responsibilities of different cybersecurity teams and knowing where different security incidents should be escalated.

## Skills Developed

- Security alert triage.
- Alert investigation.
- Incident classification.
- Escalation awareness.
- Understanding SOC responsibilities.

## Conclusion

Completing this room helped me develop a better understanding of how security alerts are managed within a SOC. It also highlighted the importance of accurate classification, communication and escalation when handling security incidents.
